# Scams
Banks and the police never ask you to move money to a safe account or share a full PIN or password. Be wary of unexpected texts, calls and messages about parcels, refunds, prizes or investments that promise high returns. If unsure, stop, hang up and call your bank using the number on your card. Report fraud to your bank and Action Fraud.

# Loan sharks and high-cost credit
Avoid unlicensed lenders and payday-style loans. Check any lender on the FCA register. If you need to borrow, ask about credit unions, which offer fairer low-cost loans, and ask about support funds first. If someone is threatening you over a loan, contact the Illegal Money Lending Team.

# Sending money abroad
Compare total cost of sending money including the exchange rate, not just the fee. Check the provider is authorised. Consider a regular fixed transfer so the cost is predictable in your budget.
